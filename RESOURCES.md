- [SLIP-0039](https://github.com/satoshilabs/slips/blob/master/slip-0039.md), Shamir's Secret-Sharing for Mnemonic Codes
- [ERC-1271](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1271), [ERC-4337](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-4337), [ERC-7579](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7579)

- NIST SP 800-63B, authentication and lifecycle management
